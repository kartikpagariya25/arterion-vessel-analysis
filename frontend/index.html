Everything Included

Built for Real Clinical Insight

Segmentation, detection and grading in one pass, with the evidence beside it.

Vessel tree segmentation

A ResNet-34 U-Net separates the 25 coronary segments of the ARCADE dataset.

Stenosis detection

A second network finds narrowed segments and returns each lesion with its bounding box.

Severity grading

Narrowing is the lesion width against the healthy neighbour, graded mild, moderate or severe.

Three-language reports

A clinician report plus patient summaries in English, Hindi and Marathi.

Video scanning

Scan an angiogram video and get the most affected frame back with its report.

Measured on held-out images

Vessel Dice 0.774 and stenosis Dice 0.594 on 300 ARCADE test images.
